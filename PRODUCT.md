# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Family members, close friends, godparents, and guests of all age groups (including older relatives) invited to Mateo's baptism. They need a simple, clear, and distraction-free interface to learn about the event details.

## Product Purpose
To serve as the central, reliable information hub for Mateo's baptism, allowing guests to view the ceremony/reception schedule, find locations, and submit their RSVPs. Success is defined by guests successfully finding details and submitting their RSVP without friction.

## Brand Personality
Serene, intimate, warm

## Anti-references
Flashy, loud, generic event templates

## Design Principles
1. **Intimate Serenity:** Visual styling should evoke a calm, personal, and warm family celebration, avoiding commercial or standard event-software aesthetics.
2. **Effortless Clarity:** Layout and visual hierarchy must make schedules and location details immediately readable.
3. **Frictionless Utility:** The RSVP submission and captcha process must be highly intuitive and completely reliable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Prioritize legibility: maintain high contrast, use clear typographic scale, and ensure form controls are easy to tap and read on both mobile and desktop screens.
